EVENT INFORMATION

Useful things to know.

Performance options

Solo piano, duo, jazz trio or a larger band can be considered depending on the event.

Timing

Event start and finish times can be confirmed before booking. Load-in and setup timing can be worked out with the venue.

Sets

When appropriate, a typical structure is about 45 minutes of music followed by a 15-minute break.

Sound & setup

Sound system, microphones, power, parking, multiple performance locations and other setup details can be discussed as needed.

Special music

Tell me about special songs, ceremony cues, holiday music or a particular style you're hoping for.

Pricing

Pricing depends on the number of musicians, performance length and event requirements. I can prepare more than one option when useful.
